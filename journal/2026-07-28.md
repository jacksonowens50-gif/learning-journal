## 6. What this replaces

Auth, paging, retries, type coercion, logging — that's what I wrote today for **one endpoint**. Fivetran and Airbyte sell that same work across ~400 connectors, maintained as vendors change their APIs.

So: when is a managed connector obviously worth it, and when would you write the twenty lines yourself?
